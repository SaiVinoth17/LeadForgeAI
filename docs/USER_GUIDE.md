# 📖 FORGE OS V6 User Guide

## Core Agency Workflows

1. **Dashboard & AI Director**:
   - Upon launching, the **AI Director Hero** displays your single highest-impact action.
   - Click `Approve & Dispatch Sales Package` to trigger automatic proposal generation.

2. **Digital Twin Client Card**:
   - Inspect Overall Health %, SEO Score %, and Buying Intent score for selected leads.

3. **Configuring AI Providers**:
   - Navigate to Settings -> AI Providers.
   - Enter your Gemini or Groq API Keys and click Save.
