# LeadForge AI - User Guide

## 1. Finding Leads
Navigate to the **Lead Generator** tab. Enter a search query (e.g., "Plumbers") and a City (e.g., "Chicago"). 
The application will use OpenStreetMap to find businesses within the radius defined in your settings. Click "Import" to save them to your CRM.

## 2. Managing the CRM
In the **CRM** tab, you can view all saved leads. 
Use the checkboxes to select multiple leads, then click **Generate Sales Package**.

## 3. The Sales Package Process
When you generate a Sales Package, LeadForge AI will automatically:
1. Scrape the business's website.
2. Analyze it for missing SEO tags, slow load times, and broken SSL.
3. Capture Desktop and Mobile screenshots using a headless browser.
4. Generate a comprehensive Proposal, Email Draft, and WhatsApp message.

## 4. Proposals
Navigate to the **Proposals** tab to view your generated pitches. You can copy them to your clipboard and paste them directly into your email client or CRM tool.
